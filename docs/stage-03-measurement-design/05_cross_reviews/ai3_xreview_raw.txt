## 3. Items requiring further targeted profiling

Each is one exact aggregate question. None is production analysis.

1. For CA_1 items in the five proposed departments, using only `wm_yr_wk ≤ 11617`, what is the joint 3×3×3 count of items by {`n_distinct_prices_pre` ≥4 vs <4} × {`n_price_changes_pre` ≥3 vs <3} × {positive weeks in last 52 ≥26 vs <26}? This sizes the design’s TE-eligible universe.
2. Under Design B §17.1 (observed-price candidates, event-filtered, ±20% band, cap 5), how many pre-origin candidate prices remain per item? Distribution: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 across the five depts.
3. Of single-week pre-origin prices at CA_1 in the five departments, what share fall in weeks where `event_type_1` ∈ {National, Religious, Sporting, Cultural} vs no event? Needed to calibrate the M-08 filter.
4. Distribution of first priced `wm_yr_wk` per CA_1 item (min, p10, median, p90, max) and count of items whose first priced week is more than 364 days before d_1941. Needed to lock U5.
5. For CA_1 items in the five departments, how many have more than 5 distinct pre-origin prices, and how many have exactly 5? Needed to confirm the §17.1 step-6 cap.
6. Distribution of `realized 28-day revenue` for the backtest window d_1914–d_1941 across trust-eligible items, by department, on the constant-price subset. Needed to define `dept_backtest_MAE_revenue` (M-09).

## 4. Items requiring a human (owner) choice between defensible alternatives

1. **Department mapping.** Alternatives: (a) five departments `{FOODS_1, FOODS_2, FOODS_3, HOUSEHOLD_1, HOUSEHOLD_2}`, exclude HOBBIES_1/2; (b) add HOBBIES to the universe but let the trust gate drop thin items; (c) restrict to FOODS_3 + HOUSEHOLD_1 (denser price history). Recommendation: (a). Both designs chose (a); the stakeholder invited a proposal and (a) best matches “everyday grocery and household.”
2. **Eligibility threshold strictness.** Alternatives: (a) Design B TE1–TE6 as written; (b) loosen TE1 to ≥3 distinct prices; (c) loosen TE2 to ≥2 changes. Recommendation: (a) initially; if Part D cross-tab (Q1 in §3 above) returns fewer than ~150 eligible items, apply (b). Return to stakeholder only if the change alters the “at least a few times” reading in T010.
3. **Current-price week.** Alternatives: (a) week 11617 (Design B); (b) week 11616 (last completed week); (c) average of 11616 and 11617. Recommendation: (a). Weekly price is the price in effect for the week that contains the decision date.
4. **Guardrail operationalization.** Alternatives: (a) hard ratio < 0.90; (b) ratio < 0.90 with a tolerance band and hold-NE near 0.90. Recommendation: (a) with the 0.85/0.95 twins reported as diagnostics.
5. **Capacity.** Alternatives: (a) hard cap 25; (b) 25 with a soft tolerance of ±2. Recommendation: (a). Stakeholder said “about 25”; hard 25 with no padding matches the intent.
6. **Candidate construction.** Alternatives: (a) observed pre-origin prices only, event-filtered, band-restricted, capped at 5 (Design B); (b) synthetic ±X% prices around current, on a disclosed grid. Recommendation: (a). It directly controls extrapolation and matches the stakeholder’s “specific price” requirement.
7. **Minimum-gain rule.** Alternatives: (a) department half-MAE revenue screen (Design B §17.3.5); (b) no screen, disclose all positive ΔR. Recommendation: (a) with the screen reported and the with/without twin; analyst-set per T040.
8. **Backtest pseudo-origin.** Alternatives: (a) d_1913 (Design B §16.3); (b) two origins d_1913 and d_1885; (c) later origin closer to the live decision. Recommendation: (a) as primary with (b) as a stability check if coverage allows.
