Resolution method. Every row is resolved from the approved decision (Stage 1), the locked question (C2), verbatim stakeholder statements (T0xx), verified data facts (P01–P04, DIP §B) and methodological principle. **No row is resolved by counting AI agreement** (framework §24, §32 "resolved by voting"). Where AIs agree, the row states the evidence that makes the agreed choice correct; where the evidence does not support a choice, the row is marked Open or Disputed.

Resolution vocabulary: **Lock** (accepted; goes into the candidate as Locked-proposed) · **Revise** (accepted with a stated change to both first passes) · **Sensitivity twin** (a prespecified diagnostic alternative, never a judged action) · **Owner choice** (defensible alternatives; the coordinator recommendation is given; the owner confirms at lock) · **Open** (not yet resolvable; recommendation given) · **Disputed** (evidence contradicts the intended resolution or the reviewers; Stage 4 branch stated).

Profile references: P01 = design_profile_01 (source facts), P02 = design_profile_02 (eligibility cells, change magnitudes), P03 = design_profile_03 (11616 vs 11617; distinct-price buckets), P04 = design_profile_04 (joint eligibility, candidate counts, single-week/event prices, first priced week, corrected recent-change counts). Qn = query n within a profile.

## Appendix A. Locked decision-rule pipeline (per universe item, live origin d_1941)

1. Universe: CA_1 × five depts (2,484 items). Every item gets exactly one row and one action.
2. Eligibility integers from prices at weeks ≤ 11617 and sales d_1577–d_1941: n_price_changes_pre, n_distinct_prices_pre, priced_weeks_pre, units_365, zero_days_365.
3. Current price P0 = week-11617 price; P0_twin = week-11616 price; straddle_flag.
4. Candidate set: distinct own prices at weeks ≤ 11617, > 0, ≠ P0, within [0.75·P0, 1.25·P0] (integer-cent comparison), minus single-week event-week prices (four types), ordered by round(abs(ln(Pc/P0)), 10) then price ascending, first 5 kept.
5. trust_eligible = e_chg3 ∧ e_u180 ∧ e_z182 ∧ e_pw52 ∧ e_cand ∧ te6 [te6 per OD-1]. Not trusted → hold_ne.
6. Fit the locked model (row 23–27); score P0 and each candidate over d_1942–d_1969; Û, R̂, ΔR̂, ρ̂.
7. Û(P0) = 0 → hold_ne. Legal candidate: ρ̂ ≥ 0.90 AND round(R̂(Pc),2) − round(R̂(P0),2) > 0. None legal → unchanged at P0. Else argmax ΔR̂ (unrounded) → raise if Pc > P0, cut if Pc < P0.
8. If backtest_accept = 0 (A1 or A2 fails at d_1913): all trust-eligible items → hold_ne.
9. Qualifiers (raise/cut) ranked by ΔR̂ desc, then pred_units_current desc, then n_price_changes_pre desc, then item_id asc; package_flag for ranks 1…min(25, n); below_line_flag for ranks > 25; no padding; holds and unchanged listed separately and do not consume slots.
