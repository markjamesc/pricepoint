## T3. OD-1 (TE6)

Choice: (ii) ADOPT WITH AMENDMENT.

Rationale. T040’s third prong needs an operational predicate or builders will invent one (RR-12). The only part of 06#8 that can be implemented *before results* and that actually means “this item’s forward 28 days is not a free guess” is the usable d_1913 slice: the item was trust-shaped at a recent origin, paid a constant price across that window, and sold at least one unit, so Û(P0_bt) can be compared to a real 28-day total. The 0.50 APE cap has no profile, no backtest, and no T0xx number; locking it would pretend evidence exists (R7). A dept-median cap is not a guess test; it fails half of usable items in every department by construction and would dominate E_A. Model-level A1/A2 (median APE ≤ 0.40, bias in [−0.20, +0.20], U=0 excluded) already kill every modeled action if the instrument is uncalibrated; stacking an unevidenced item cap on top is not proportionate. Items with no usable slice fail TE6 → hold_ne. APE_i remains an audit field.

Complete exact rule text (status becomes Locked-proposed at lock if the owner accepts this audit):

TE6 — “forward four-week expectation is not a guess.”

TE6 passes if and only if the item has a usable d_1913 backtest slice. A usable slice means all of:
1. E_A conjuncts true when re-anchored at origin d_1913: n_price_changes counted on weeks ≤ 11613; units_365 summed on d_1549–d_1913; zero_days_365 counted on d_1549–d_1913; priced_weeks counted on weeks ≤ 11613; n_candidates ≥ 1 built from prices at weeks ≤ 11613 under the locked band/event/cap rules with P0_bt = week-11613 price.
2. Actual sell_price is the same number in each of weeks 11613, 11614, 11615, 11616, and 11617.
3. Realized units summed over d_1914–d_1941 > 0.

Items without a usable slice: `te6 = 0` → `trust_eligible = 0` → action = hold_ne.

No item-level APE threshold is applied. In particular, APE_i ≤ 0.50 and APE_i ≤ department-median APE are not TE6 conjuncts. APE_i = abs(Û_i(P0_bt) − U_i) / U_i is computed on the usable set, written to the audit table, and summarized by department as a diagnostic.

Interaction with 06#28: if `backtest_accept = 0` (A1 or A2 fails on the locked acceptance set), every item that would otherwise be trust-eligible is assigned hold_ne. Collapse overrides TE6, raise, cut, and model-based unchanged. d_1885 does not enter TE6.

Label: provisional policy operationalization of T040 prong 3, disclosed before execution, not an SLA.
