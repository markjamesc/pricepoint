B. Price-change magnitude — do I accept AI 1’s deferral?

**No, I do not accept deferring price-change magnitude as merely Framing/Stage 3.** The stakeholder would have to answer: **When you say “raise the shelf price” or “take a targeted price cut,” should the recommendation include a specific new shelf price or price-change amount for each product, or only the direction, with the amount chosen later by the pricing team? If only direction, how should “compared with the current price” and “biggest revenue gain” be interpreted?**

This cannot be only a Framing or Stage 3 choice because T014 says Morgan needs to know what to expect “at the current price versus at the price I’d be recommending,” and T024 defines “strongest” as “the biggest revenue gain that you actually trust.” Revenue gain and ranking require a defined counterfactual price. Stage 3 can choose how to estimate or optimize the magnitude, but it cannot decide whether magnitude is part of Morgan’s recommendation in the first place. That changes the decision object: “which products should be raised/cut” versus “what price should be recommended for each product.”
