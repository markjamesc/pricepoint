## SECTION 2 — INTERNAL NOTE (not sent)

**Start Gate status:** All Start Gate elements are now established. Moving toward final stakeholder confirmation of the decision statement.

### Established

**Decision owner**

* Morgan Lee, Pricing & Revenue Manager, makes the recommendation.
* Store general manager signs off.

**Action or choice**

* Raise shelf price.
* Take a targeted price cut.
* Leave shelf price unchanged.
* Hold — not enough evidence.

**Outcome**

* Maximize expected product revenue over the following 28 days.
* Revenue = units sold × shelf price.
* Margin is not used because cost data is unavailable.

**Material constraints**

* Reject recommendations expected to reduce units by more than approximately 10%.
* Do not force weak evidence into a price-change recommendation.
* Prefer fewer stronger recommendations.
* Actual price-change package capacity is approximately 25 per four-week cycle.

**Capacity and prioritization**

* The analysis produces a ranked list.
* Top ~25 raise/cut recommendations go into the GM package.
* Additional qualifying products appear below the line as next in line.
* No padding if fewer than 25 qualify.
* Ranking objective established:

  * strongest = biggest expected revenue gain that is trusted.
* Exact scoring method delegated to analysis with disclosure.

**Product/store grain**

* Product at a specific store.
* First round:

  * California pilot store.
  * Everyday grocery and household aisles.

**Eligibility principle**

* Products require:

  * actual historical shelf-price movement,
  * sufficient sales activity.
* Exact cutoffs delegated to later analytical design with rationale.

**Time scope**

* Four-week pricing cycle.
* Evaluation horizon: 28 days after change.
* Decision point: end of most recent available history.
* No look-ahead beyond information available at that time.

**Information available at decision time**
Available:

* Daily unit sales.
* Weekly shelf prices.
* Calendar information:

  * holidays,
  * events,
  * SNAP benefit days.

Unavailable:

* Competitor prices.
* Inventory.
* Cost/margin.

**Uncertainty tolerance**

* Fewer recommendations with stronger evidence.
* Hold — not enough evidence is legitimate.

### Missing

None that block Start.

Potential items to defer:

* Exact eligibility cutoffs.
* Exact scoring formula for ranking trusted revenue gain.
* Exact definition of “not enough evidence.”
* Whether a recommendation includes price-change magnitude.

These are analytical design choices for later stages because Morgan explicitly delegated how to score and define cutoffs while requiring disclosure.
